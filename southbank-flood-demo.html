About this prototype

A local route-planning demonstration for a small Southbank area. The walking network and map geometry come from an OpenStreetMap snapshot; flooding is entirely simulated.

Routes follow the selected connected public walking network. Clicked points snap to this network. The displayed distance starts at the snapped point and does not include movement from a building or the original click location.

Any mapped segment touching the simulated flood is closed. Remaining routes minimise walking distance. A result of “no route” applies only within this demo network. Paths and crossings have not been verified by a field walk.
